## Interpretation notes (written after grading; no verdict changed)

- **D1 fails on detection, not location.** Of the 240 bright planted arc/streak/pair/cloud features, 55 were flagged at all (23%) and 43 were flagged and located; 151 had the reported position within 5″ of the feature regardless of flag. Even at signal-to-noise 160, 27 of 80 were hits (arcs 14/20, streaks 8/20, clouds 4/20, pairs 1/20). Identical pairs and single-band clouds are almost never flagged.
- **D3 passes mainly because artefacts are rarely flagged.** Only 23 of 200 planted artefacts were flagged; 18 were flagged and located, and 12 of those (8 of 13 at brightness ≥ 40) were given a non-artefact nature with medium or high confidence. Conditional on being noticed, an artefact is more often explained as astrophysics than not; D3 as pre-registered measures the unconditional rate.
- **Flag rate drift.** The cut (interest ≥ 2.6) flagged 4% of untouched development objects (the rule's target is ≤ 5%) but 9.0% of untouched test S_R, 10.5% of S_B and 34.5% of grade A lenses. D2 (specific false features, 9.0%) passes narrowly; most test flags carry a specific feature label.
- **D5 is at the FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE boundary** (58.5%, interval 55–62%): counts agree 70%, elongation 47%, angle 68%, colour 54%.
- **Chance location** at 5″ is about 17% for these extended footprints (development estimate), not the spec's 8%.
- **Not run:** runs D (batching, D11) and E (hint, D9) and the last 324 calls of run B were stopped by the frozen quota rule; D6b uses 476 run-B objects. Mergers: no catalogue object reaches `merging_merger` 0.5 (set skipped). Known lensed quasars: 7 usable of 9 (3 flagged).
